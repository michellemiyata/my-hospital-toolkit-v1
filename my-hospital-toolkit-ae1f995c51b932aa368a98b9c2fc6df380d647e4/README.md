# My Hospital Toolkit — Physical A5 Booklet & Digital Companion

**Making Hospital Information Easier to Understand** — A unified UX/UI and service design prototype combining an **8-page printable A5 patient information booklet** with an optional **QR-linked accessible digital web application**.

## Included Formats in This Repository

1. **Interactive Web Prototype & Booklet Flipbook (`index.html`)**
   - **Interactive 8-Page A5 Booklet Preview**: Flip through facing pages on desktop or single pages on mobile.
   - **Print & A4 Imposition View**: Supports both double-sided A4 saddle-stitch imposition (`[8|1]`, `[2|7]`, `[6|3]`, `[4|5]`) for folding and stapling, and sequential A5 pages (`1–8`).
   - **Digital Toolkit + Hospital Wayfinding Map**: Includes step-by-step visit guide, color-coded hospital wayfinding directory, tap-to-speak Talk Cards, interactive checklists, Easy Info cards, Larger Text (`A+`), and High Contrast mode.
   - **Configurable QR Code**: Dynamically generates a real scannable ISO QR code on Page 1 (Front Cover) and Page 8 (Back Cover) when a deployed URL is entered.

2. **Print-Ready PDFs**
   - `My-Hospital-Toolkit-A5-Booklet.pdf` — All 8 sequential A5 pages ($148.5 \times 210\text{ mm}$).
   - `My-Hospital-Toolkit-A4-Imposed-Print.pdf` — 4 A4 landscape faces imposed for double-sided printing, folding in half, and stapling along the spine.
